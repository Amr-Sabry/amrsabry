STEP 03

Storyboards.

Black and white sketch pass, in two routes. Pick an option, then a film, to see its frames.

Option B: the pack comes first

Built on your sequence. Every 8 second film opens on the closed pack, the pack opens, the flavour bursts out, everything returns into the pack, and we end on the pack again with the tagline. The product is on screen at the start and at the end.

The 15 second film

  • Opens on the four closed packs together.
  • Each pack opens for a 2.5 second flavour burst.
  • Everything returns to the packs. Line-up, logo and مررره يمي.

Option A: the ingredient comes first

Our first route, kept for comparison. Every 6 second film starts on the ingredient, then the chip takes the flavour, the pack arrives and the pack shot lands with the tagline. The pack is only seen in the second half.

The 15 second film

  • Opens on four chips falling, one per flavour.
  • Each flavour gets a 2.5 second beat in its own colour world.
  • Ends on the four packs together with the logo and مررره يمي.
