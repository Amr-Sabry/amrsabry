STEP 02

Storyboard.

Black and white sketch pass. Composition, action and rhythm only. No colour, no lighting, no final design yet.

The idea: some snacks whisper. Ours never learned how.

A silent room. One pack. The moment it opens, the crunch becomes a visible shockwave that wakes up everything around it and pulls everyone in. The pack and the chip are the heroes, the sound is the story.

Why it fits Yummis

  • Built on the brand's own signature: you hear it before you taste it.
  • Four flavours get one clean beauty beat each, in their own colour world.
  • Ends on sharing, the "Sociable" trait, and the consumer tagline.
