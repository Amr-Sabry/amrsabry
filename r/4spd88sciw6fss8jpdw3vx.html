STEP 03

Storyboards.

Black and white sketch pass. Composition, action and rhythm only. Pick a film to see its frames.

The pack comes first

Built on your sequence. Every 8 second film opens on the closed pack, the pack opens, the flavour bursts out, the hero chip snaps cleanly in two with no crumbs, everything returns into the pack, and we end on the pack again with the tagline.

The 15 second film

  • Opens on the chips falling, one per flavour.
  • Each pack flies through its own flavour for 2.8 seconds, then one clean chip snap.
  • Ends on the packs together with the logo and مررره يمي.

Option A: the ingredient comes first

Our first route, kept for comparison. Every 6 second film starts on the ingredient, then the chip takes the flavour, the pack arrives and the pack shot lands with the tagline. The pack is only seen in the second half.

The 15 second film

  • Opens on the chips falling, one per flavour.
  • Each flavour gets a 2.5 second beat in its own colour world.
  • Ends on the packs together with the logo and مررره يمي.
