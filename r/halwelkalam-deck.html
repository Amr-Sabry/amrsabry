Intro

BEYOND THE BUZZ.

Because what we say matters as much as what we watch.

حلو الكلام is a contemporary late night entertainment show that connects generations, with a strong focus on youth and the social, cultural, and digital influences shaping their everyday lives.

Through humor, satire, trending stories, and authentic conversations, the show goes beyond reflecting Egyptian society. It aims to offer fresh perspectives, encourage positive values, and explore possible solutions all woven naturally into entertainment, without preaching or judgment.

Segment 1: Trending News Desk

WHAT’S TRENDING TONIGHT?

A fast-paced, comedy-driven news desk inspired by Jon Stewart and iconic late-night shows, where the host (ideally Akram Hosny) delivers a sharp, satirical take on trending stories, current events, and viral moments, brought to life through creative visuals, graphics, and sound design.

The segment also tackles fake news and misinformation, using humor and fact-checking to expose the truth behind viral stories. Extending beyond the studio, two prerecorded comedy sketches creatively explore the episode’s biggest trends, including how fake news is created, exaggerated, and spread.
